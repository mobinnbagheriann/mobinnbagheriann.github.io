I am a final-year undergraduate student in Computer Engineering at Sharif University of Technology. I am currently a research intern at the Brain x Machine Intelligence Lab @ KAIST , supervised by Prof. Sang Wan Lee, and a research assistant at the Sharif Machine Learning Lab, supervised by Prof. Mahdieh Soleymani Baghshah.

So far, I have worked mostly on mechanistic interpretability of language and vision-language models, especially counting, grounding, object binding, and causal analysis of internal representations.

More broadly, I am interested in how learned systems develop internal structure rich enough to support flexible behavior, and how simple rules and objectives can give rise to complex forms of intelligence.
